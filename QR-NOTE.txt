M-PESA QR MVP
================
Bundled QR: images/mpesa-uchumi-till-893226.png
Merchant Till: 893226

This MVP QR carries the receiver/till identifier only. The M-PESA app should
prompt for the amount after scanning. A dynamic amount QR is a separate
Safaricom/Daraja Dynamic QR integration and should be implemented once
Uchumi provides the required Daraja access/credentials.
